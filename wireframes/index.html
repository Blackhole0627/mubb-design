9. Motion and behavior

pageFade + slight rise on entering a page; header turns solid black after scrolling past the hero.
imagesReveal on scroll (fade, 8 px rise); slow zoom on hero (1.00 → 1.05). Project cards lift and brighten on hover.
carouselsDrag / swipe / arrow buttons, snap points, no autoplay.
videoHero video muted and looping, poster image while loading; full video opens in a lightbox.
navFull-screen overlay menu on mobile.
rulesRespect prefers-reduced-motion; all durations in tokens.css; images served as AVIF/WebP at the right size (photo-heavy pages must stay fast).
